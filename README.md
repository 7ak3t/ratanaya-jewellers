# RATANAYA JEWELLERS — Website Starter

A first-pass luxury jewellery website structure based on the supplied Miss Jo, Elinor Jewels and Tiffany references.

## Design language
- Minimal luxury / editorial typography
- Warm ivory neutrals with a restrained burgundy accent
- Large whitespace and product-first imagery
- Clean catalogue cards
- Indian warmth primarily in campaign/story sections
- Authentic jewellery remains the locked product reference

## SEO foundation
Homepage title/description, canonical URL, robots meta, Open Graph, Twitter card, JewelryStore JSON-LD placeholder, robots.txt and sitemap.xml are included.

## Before launch
Replace the placeholder domain, social links and business information; add real product data, product schema, image alt text, analytics, search, wishlist, cart, checkout and policies.

## Next production step
Convert this into Next.js + TypeScript with reusable Header, MegaMenu, ProductCard, ProductGallery, ProductPage, CartDrawer and SEO components. Then connect the real RATANAYA catalogue and approved product photography.
